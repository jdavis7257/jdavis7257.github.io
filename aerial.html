Austin from Above

Aerial stock footage of Austin, Texas

Austin's sprawl of glass towers against the Hill Country horizon makes it one of the most visually striking cities to capture from the air. This collection includes drone and aerial footage shot over Austin's most recognizable locations — from wide establishing shots of the downtown skyline reflecting off Lady Bird Lake to low-altitude passes over the Congress Avenue Bridge, the Texas State Capitol dome, and the winding curves of Pennybacker Bridge at sunset.

Clips are available in 4K and HD resolution, covering a range of conditions: golden hour aerials with warm light raking across the city, blue hour twilight with the skyline lit up, and clear midday footage for documentary and editorial use. Whether you need an opening establishing shot for a corporate video, b-roll for a real estate presentation, or scenic footage for a travel piece, these clips are ready to license and download immediately.

All aerial footage is shot on professional-grade drone equipment and is available royalty-free through Pond5 with standard and extended licensing options for commercial, editorial, and personal projects.
