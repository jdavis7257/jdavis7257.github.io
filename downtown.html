Street Level

Downtown Austin, Texas stock footage

Downtown Austin is where the city's identity comes into sharpest focus — the mix of historic Texas architecture and sleek new development, the energy of Congress Avenue, the neon glow of 6th Street at night, and the quieter residential character of Rainey Street's converted bungalows. For video producers and content creators, downtown Austin offers some of the most versatile and recognizable b-roll in Texas.

This collection includes street-level footage shot throughout downtown Austin. You'll find walking-perspective clips along Congress Avenue looking toward the Capitol, evening footage of the 6th Street entertainment district, the high-rise canyon of 2nd and 3rd Streets near the convention center, and the tree-lined blocks of the Warehouse District. Coverage includes both daytime and nighttime conditions, with clips capturing foot traffic, vehicle movement, architecture details, and the general atmosphere of each neighborhood.

Downtown Austin footage is particularly useful for corporate videos, tourism marketing, real estate presentations, documentary work, and any project that needs to establish an Austin, Texas setting. All clips are available royalty-free through Pond5 in 4K and HD.
