Lady Bird Lake is the centerpiece of many Austin establishing shots, with its kayakers, stand-up paddleboarders, and the hike-and-bike trail running along both shores beneath the downtown skyline. The Congress Avenue Bridge — famous for the largest urban bat colony in North America — provides dramatic footage opportunities at sunset as the bats emerge. The Texas State Capitol building, with its distinctive pink granite dome, is essential for any content related to Texas government, politics, or history. And Pennybacker Bridge, also known as the 360 Bridge, is arguably Austin's most photographed structure — the steel arch spanning Lake Austin against a backdrop of Hill Country limestone cliffs.

Additional landmark coverage includes Mount Bonnell, Zilker Park, Barton Springs, and South Congress Avenue. All footage and photography is shot on location and available royalty-free through Pond5 for commercial, editorial, and personal licensing.
